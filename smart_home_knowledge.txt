A smart home is a residence equipped with smart devices such as lights, locks, thermostats, and sensors that can be controlled remotely or automatically.

Smart devices allow users to automate tasks, reduce energy consumption, and control appliances via voice, app, or assistant.

You can control the following devices and locations in your smart home:

Devices: lights, locks, thermostat  
Locations: kitchen, bedroom, living_room, kids_room, bathroom, front_door, all

---

Here are example commands you can use to operate your smart home devices:

- Turn on the kitchen light by saying: "turn on the kitchen light"
- Turn off the kitchen lights by saying: "turn off the kitchen lights"
- Turn on all lights by saying: "turn on all lights"
- Turn off all lights by saying: "turn off all lights"
- To dim the lights, say: "dim the bedroom lights"
- Lock the front door by saying: "lock the front door"
- Unlock the front door by saying: "unlock the front door"
- Set the thermostat to a temperature by saying: "set the thermostat to 22 degrees in the living room"
- To check if any lights are on, ask: "Are any of the lights on?"

---

Please use device names exactly as listed above (e.g., lights, lock, thermostat) and location names as shown (e.g., kitchen, bedroom, kids_room).

For all-room commands, use the location "all" (e.g., "turn off all lights").

---

Safety notes:

- Certain commands like turning off all lights after 10 PM may require confirmation.
- Commands to disable security devices or open doors for strangers are blocked for safety.
